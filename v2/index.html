3. Edit card text and give consent

    
Pattern redaction covers only common forms of phone numbers, emails, amounts and addresses. Check for names or addresses, especially in the Before and After lines. Cards quote only the reviewed Before and After utterances, not other lines or redaction markers. Editing the text clears consent.
